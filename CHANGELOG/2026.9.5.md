- **`plugin-state-sync-keyed-store`** was deprecated on September 11, 2026. Migrate to `api.runtime.state.openKeyedStore` and `PluginStateKeyedStore`, awaiting operations while keeping transaction callbacks synchronous. The synchronous adapter remains supported until a future Plugin SDK major and an explicitly approved migration. See `/plugins/sdk-runtime/state-and-system#synchronous-keyed-store-migration`.

- **`sdk-untrusted-context-identifier-aliases`** remains removal-pending after its September 8, 2026 deadline. Published-plugin reader migration and explicit breaking-release approval are still required before removal; use the channel-named context fields and `buildChannelMetadata` for new code. See `/plugins/compatibility`.
